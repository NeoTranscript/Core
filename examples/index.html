NeoTranscript

Students own their official transcripts and diplomas using Verifiable Credentials.

No more gatekeepers. Full control + cryptographic proof.

Live Demo Flow

1. Issuer

Create rich college transcripts or high school diplomas

2. Student Wallet

View your credential + selective disclosure (choose what to share)

3. Verifier

Anyone can instantly verify the credential

Current Capabilities

  • ✅ Full course-level transcripts (dynamic builder + quick CSV paste)
  • ✅ High School Diploma support
  • ✅ Student-controlled selective disclosure (hide GPA, specific courses, etc.)
  • ✅ Real W3C Verifiable Credential structure
  • ✅ Cryptographic proof (Ed25519Signature2020 simulation)
  • ✅ Works 100% in browser — no install required
  • ✅ Mobile-friendly
